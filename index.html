About Ryan.

Chicago-born. Midwestern at heart. Curious by nature.

25+
Years across technology, finance & consulting

Ryan A. Lopez is a technology and business professional whose work spans corporate IT, finance, consulting, software testing, project management, business analytics and enterprise content management. Over more than 25 years, he has worked across complex business environments, including Big Five consulting, and founded two consulting firms: Catalyses and Aspire Technologies. Both are now closed, but each remains an important chapter in his entrepreneurial story.

Ryan graduated with distinction in both Finance and Information Decision Sciences at the University of Illinois at Chicago. He is a Chicago-born, lifelong Illinoian who values music, travel, food, photography, hiking, nature, art and culture—and is a proud father.
